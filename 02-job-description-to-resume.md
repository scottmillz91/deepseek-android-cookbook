## Workflow 2: Job description → Resume bullets

**Use case:** You found a job you want, but you need to tailor your resume to match their exact keywords.
**Time saved:** ~20 minutes
**Android steps:** Open DeepSeek → paste the prompt and the job description → copy result → paste into your resume.

**Prompt:**
> I am applying for the following job: [Paste Job Description]. Here is a list of my current skills and experience: [Paste your brief resume or list of skills]. Based on the job description, give me 3 tailored resume bullet points using action verbs. Focus on the skills they are asking for.

**Example input:** Job Description: "Looking for a customer service rep with experience in Zendesk, conflict resolution, and handling high-volume ticket queues." My skills: "I worked at a retail store for 3 years answering phones and helping angry customers."

**Example output:**
*   Resolved high-volume customer inquiries and complaints, maintaining a calm, solution-oriented approach during peak retail hours.
*   Managed conflict resolution and de-escalated frustrated customers, ensuring a positive experience and repeat business.
*   Quickly adapted to new software systems (ready to master Zendesk) to track and organize customer requests efficiently.

**Limitations:** Always double-check that the output is 100% truthful and accurately reflects your actual experience.
