## Workflow 3: Long article → Study notes

**Use case:** You have a dense article, PDF text, or textbook chapter to read, but you need to study it fast.
**Time saved:** ~30 minutes
**Android steps:** Open DeepSeek → copy and paste the text of the article → get instant notes.

**Prompt:**
> Read the following text and create study notes for me. Provide: 1. A one-sentence summary. 2. The top 3 key takeaways. 3. Three flashcards (Question and Answer format) based on the most important facts. Here is the text: [Paste your text here]

**Example input:** "The mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell. It generates most of the chemical energy needed to power the cell's biochemical reactions. Chemical energy produced by the mitochondria is stored in a small molecule called adenosine triphosphate (ATP)."

**Example output:**
**One-sentence summary:** The mitochondria generates and stores chemical energy for the cell in the form of ATP.
**Top 3 Key Takeaways:**
1. Mitochondria are known as the powerhouse of the cell.
2. They generate the cell's chemical energy.
3. The energy is stored in a molecule called ATP.
**Flashcards:**
*   Q: What is the main function of the mitochondria? A: To generate most of the cell's chemical energy.
*   Q: How is the chemical energy stored? A: In a small molecule called adenosine triphosphate (ATP).

**Limitations:** If the text is too long, DeepSeek might time out. Try breaking long PDFs into smaller chunks.
