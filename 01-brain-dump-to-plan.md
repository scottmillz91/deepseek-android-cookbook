## Workflow 1: Messy brain-dump → 3-task plan

**Use case:** You have too much to do and need a clear next step.
**Time saved:** ~10 minutes
**Android steps:** Open DeepSeek → paste the prompt → copy result into notes.

**Prompt:**
> Here’s everything on my mind: I need to clean the kitchen, reply to two emails, buy groceries, call my mom, finish a homework assignment, do laundry, and figure out what to make for dinner. Turn this into a realistic plan for today with only 3 tasks. For each task, give me the first 10-minute action.

**Example input:** I need to clean the kitchen, reply to two emails, buy groceries, call my mom, finish a homework assignment, do laundry, and figure out what to make for dinner.

**Example output:**
Today’s 3 tasks:

1. Finish the homework assignment (60–90 min) — First 10-minute action: Open the assignment, write down the exact deliverable and deadline, then break it into 3 small steps. Start step 1 only.
2. Reply to the two emails (15 min) — First 10-minute action: Open your inbox, search for the two emails, and draft one short reply. Send it, then draft and send the second.
3. Plan dinner + buy groceries (45–60 min) — First 10-minute action: Pick one simple dinner, list 5–7 ingredients, check what you already have, and make a grocery list. Then go shop.

Park for later: kitchen cleaning, laundry, and calling your mom. If you finish early, batch the call and laundry as a bonus—but don’t add them to today’s core plan.

**Limitations:** It can't know your calendar; check deadlines yourself.
